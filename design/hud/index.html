ATHLETE HUD

The top stripe stays vanilla: both teams, games won in the series, kills, towers, gold and the clock. The athlete HUD lives in the bottom stripe plus one skill row above it, and it is read through icons, bars and numbers rather than words. Hover a skill or item, on the frame or in the 1:1 band, to see its tooltip.
